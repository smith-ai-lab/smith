# Clang builtins (`front/types_builtin.smith`, `front/emit_builtin.smith`, #2286, part of #2224)

std spells a few operations the way clang does, and the checker knows them as intrinsics. The names stay as clang spells them (`__builtin_add_overflow(a, b, &r)`): they are std's private machinery, with no new-syntax alias. `types_builtin::find` is the table: each `Entry` has a `Shape` (the parameter and result types, checked by `types_builtin::check`) and the LLVM intrinsic it lowers to. In `types_expr::call_before` a call whose callee is a plain name of the table that no scope declares is typed in stage 10 (`builtin_after`); the type argument of `__builtin_bit_cast(T, x)` is read as a type, not typed as a value. `emit_call::call` hands the same calls to `emit_builtin::call`. Covered: `__builtin_{add,sub,mul}_overflow` (`llvm.{s,u}op.with.overflow`, the wrapped result stored through the pointer, `Bool` result), `__builtin_bit_cast` (through a temporary), `__builtin_assume`, `__builtin_trap`, `__builtin_elementwise_{abs,min,minnum,maxnum,sqrt,sin,cos,pow}`, `__builtin_{fabs,fabsf,sqrt,floor,floorf}`, `__builtin_{isnan,isfinite}`, `__builtin_{va_start,va_end,vsnprintf}` (see the variadic line of front::emit) and `__builtin_frame_address(0)` (`llvm.frameaddress`). The libc-shaped ones are one shape, `libc`, whose entry text is `<libc name> <parameter letters> <result letter>` (`p` pointer, `z` 64-bit unsigned, `i` int, `d`/`f` float; result `p` is the first argument's type, `v` is Void): `__builtin_{memcpy,memmove,memset,memcmp,strlen,strcmp,strncmp,strcpy,strncpy,strchr,strstr,strcspn,ldexp,nextafterf}` lower to a call of the libc function (declared on first use, integers cast to `i32`/`i64`), and `__builtin_clzll` to `llvm.ctlz`. Not yet: the variadic ones (`snprintf`, `vsnprintf`, `va_*`), an array as a pointer argument, `types_compatible_p`, `frame_address`. To add one: a row in `find`, its shape in `check` and its lowering in `emit_builtin::lowered`; tests: `tests/front_builtin_test.smith`.
